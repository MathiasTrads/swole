SWOLE

Privacy note

Pilot version · October 2026

Swole is a training app in a small, invite-only pilot. This note explains what we store about you, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: we keep only what we need to plan your training, only you and your trainer see it, and you can download or delete all of it at any time.

Who is responsible

The Swole pilot is run by Mathias Trads in Copenhagen, Denmark. Questions or requests: hello@joinswole.com.

What we store

  • Your email address (to log you in with a code).
  • Your goal and your answers in the sign-up, including injuries or health issues you tell us about.
  • Your plan, sessions, test results and the notes you write.
  • Your daily check-ins: sleep, energy, soreness, stress, food, and body weight if you log it.
  • Your Swole and Locker choices.

Some of this is health data. We only use it because you agree to it in the app.

Why we use it

  • To build your plan and adjust it to how you feel each day.
  • To keep training safe, for example easing off when you report pain or poor recovery.
  • So your trainer can follow and guide you.
  • To learn how to make Swole better during the pilot.

We don't sell your data, show you ads, or share it with anyone for their own use.

Who can see it

You, and the trainer you train with in Swole. Trainer access is part of taking part in the pilot. Other members don't see your check-ins or logs.

We use a few services to run the app, and they only handle data on our behalf:

  • Supabase stores your account and data on servers in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Brevo sends the login code emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app itself. It doesn't receive your training data.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account, or until the pilot ends. When the pilot ends, we delete pilot data within 30 days unless you've moved on to the full version of Swole.

Your rights

  • Get a copy: Settings → Your data → Download my data.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Your data → Delete my account. It's immediate and permanent.
  • Withdraw your consent: delete your account, or email us.
  • Correct or ask about your data: email us.
  • Complain: you can contact the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk.

Questions? Email hello@joinswole.com. We'll update this note if anything changes, and ask for your OK again if the change matters.
